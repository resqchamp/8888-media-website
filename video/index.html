What I Shoot

01

Engagement & Save The Date Film

An hour or two with you somewhere that means something. People book this for two different moments — the day you tell everyone you're engaged, and the date card that goes out with the invitations. It's the same shoot either way; the edit is what changes.

Wedding Film

Eight hours, from getting ready through the first dances. One filmmaker, two cameras, and microphones on the ceremony so you can hear what was actually said.

  • Six weeks

Wedding Film, Extended

Ten hours and a longer edit, for a day with more in it — a separate ceremony and reception, a large party, or speeches you want kept whole.

How It Works

  1. FIRSTWe talk. Fifteen minutes on the phone about the day, the venue, and what you actually want to remember.
  2. THENI send pricing, and you decide. A deposit holds the date; the balance is due the week of.
  3. A MONTH OUTWe go over the timeline, and I'll talk to your photographer so we aren't standing in each other's shots.
  4. ON THE DAYI'll give you a little direction. Most of the day I stay out of the way, but I'll borrow the two of you for a few minutes to find better light and tell you what to do with your hands. That's usually where the shot you end up loving comes from.
  5. AFTERYou get the film by the date above, as a private link you can send to anyone.
