The Project

A new website for Dividing Creek Fire & Rescue to help recruit volunteers, share events and fundraisers, and make it easy for the community to support the company, built from the design members have already previewed:

Preview: www.8888media.co/dividing-creek-fire-rescue

What’s Included

  • A custom one-page website with the company’s own photos and details: what the company does, joining the company, events, supporting the company, and finding the station. Works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • A “Join the Company” interest form that sends each response to the company’s email.
  • Events that update automatically from the company’s Google Calendar. Officers add events from their phones, with no logins or website skills needed.
  • Built to WCAG 2.1 Level AA, the accessibility standard named in the federal ADA rule for state and local government websites.
  • The website and web address are set up in an account owned by the company. 8888 Media works as an invited helper whose access can be removed at any time.
  • Two rounds of changes before launch, and free fixes for 30 days after.
  • Submitted to Google so the site can appear in search results.
