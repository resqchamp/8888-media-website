What We Do

Answering the Call in 2024

Every member of Dividing Creek Fire & Rescue is a volunteer. When the tones drop, they leave dinner, work and bed to help the people they live next to.

141calls answered in 2024
26volunteer members
9:24average minutes to arrive
77times we backed up neighboring companies

Fire

Structure, brush and vehicle fires across Dividing Creek and Downe Township.

Rescue

Crashes, entrapments and people who need help getting out of harm’s way.

Medical Assists

Most of our calls: arriving fast to give basic life support until the ambulance crew takes over.

Mutual Aid

When a neighboring company needs more hands, our trucks roll to help them too.

Volunteer

Join the Company

You don’t need experience, just the willingness to learn and show up. We’ll get you the training for the role you choose.

  • Firefighter

    Train on the trucks and gear, then respond to fires and rescues with the crew.

  • Rescue and Medical

    Help on the calls we run most: medical assists alongside the ambulance crews.

  • Support Member

    Not riding the trucks? Fundraisers, events and keeping the station running need people too.
