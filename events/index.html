02

Sponsor & Fundraising Film

Built to do a job, not to commemorate. Interviews with the people who run it and the people it helps, cut to two minutes you can put in front of a sponsor or at the top of a donation page.

  • Interview-led
  • 90 seconds to 2 minutes
  • Captions burned in
  • Shot across one or two days
  • Three weeks
03

Highlight Reel

Short, fast and made for a phone. For teams, leagues and brands who need something to post the same week rather than a film to keep forever.

  • Half a day on site
  • Three 30–45 second cuts
  • Vertical and widescreen
  • One week

Anything here can be scaled up or down — a second camera, a longer film, extra cuts for each sponsor. Send me the date and I'll send pricing the same day.
