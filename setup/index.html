Set Up Your Website Account

Three short steps in an account that belongs to you. Then I build and publish your site.

About 15 minutes Done once You own everything Nothing to install

How This Works

Your website will live in a free Cloudflare account, and that account is yours, in your name. Cloudflare is the company that will run your site and look after your web address.

You create the account, get your web address ready (buy a new one, or add the one you already have), and add me as a helper. You stay in charge the whole time. I just do the building.

Before You Start

  • Your email inbox open on the same device, since you'll get a verification email.
  • Buying a new web address? A debit or credit card (roughly $10–12 a year) and two or three ideas for the name, in case your first choice is taken.
  • Already have one? Just know which company you bought it from, like GoDaddy, Squarespace or Wix. You’ll need that login for one short step later, not today.

Your Three Steps

  1. 1

    Create Your Free Account

    2 minutes

    Go to dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up and sign up with your email and a password. The free plan is all you need, and it won’t ask for a card at this point.

    Don’t Skip This

    Cloudflare emails you a verification link. Click it before you do anything else. Until your email is verified, you can’t set up your address or add me in the next steps, and the screen won’t always tell you why.

  2. 2

    Get Your Web Address Ready

    5 minutes

    Do you already have a web address for your business?

    Buying a New Address

    While you’re signed in, open this link. It goes straight to the page for buying an address:

    dash.cloudflare.com → Register Domains

    1. Type the name you want and select Search.
    2. Next to the one you want, select Purchase.
    3. Leave the length at 1 year. It renews automatically, and you can change that later.
    4. Fill in your contact details. This is what makes the address legally yours. Your personal details are kept off the public record wherever the rules allow it.
    5. Add your card, read the agreements, and select Complete Purchase.

    Cloudflare charges exactly what the address costs, with no markup, so it’s usually cheaper than the places you’ve heard of. It’s ready in under a minute.

    Check the Spelling Twice

    Web address purchases can’t be refunded. The money goes to the registry the moment you confirm, so a typo means paying again. Short, easy to say out loud, .com, and no hyphens is almost always the right choice.

    Adding the Address You Already Have

    You keep your address right where it is. This adds it to your new account so your site can use it. Your email and any website you have now keep working the whole time.

    1. In your Cloudflare dashboard, open Domains, then select Onboard a domain. If your menu looks different, type “Onboard a domain” into the dashboard’s search bar.
    2. Type your address the short way, like yourbusiness.com with no “www”. Leave the other choices as they are and select Continue.
    3. Choose the Free plan and continue.
    4. Cloudflare copies your address’s current settings and shows you a list. Don’t change anything. Just continue.
    5. Cloudflare then shows you two nameservers and asks you to change them where you bought your address. Stop here. That’s this step done.

    Stop Before the Nameservers

    Don’t change anything at GoDaddy, Squarespace or wherever you bought your address yet. I’ll check your email and website settings first, then send you that last step with exact instructions. Doing it in that order keeps your email working.

  3. 3

    Add Me as a Helper

    2 minutes
    1. In your Cloudflare dashboard, open Manage Account, then Members. If your menu looks different, type “Members” into the dashboard’s search bar.
    2. Select Invite.
    3. Enter mike@8888media.co.
    4. Give access to your whole account, not just one address, and choose the Administrator role.
    5. Select Continue to Summary, then Invite.

    Then send me a quick text or email so I know to accept it.

    What This Lets Me Do

    • Build your website
    • Publish it to your address
    • Manage the site’s settings

    What It Doesn’t

    • See or change your billing
    • Add or remove anyone
    • Take the account from you
  4. ✓

    That’s Your Part Done

    I’ll build your site inside your account, connect your address, and send you a preview link before anything goes live.

    If you already had an address, I’ll also send you one short last step that points it at your new account. It takes about five minutes, and I’ll walk you through it.

    Later on, you’ll get one more email from Cloudflare asking you to verify your email address. That’s how messages from your website’s contact form, and your new info@ address, reach your inbox. Click the link in it, and you’re completely set.

Questions You Might Have

I already have a web address. Do I need a new one?

No, keep it. In step 2, choose Yes, I Already Have One. You’ll keep paying for it wherever you bought it, and your email keeps working the whole time.

What happens to the website I have now?

It stays up while I build. When your new site is ready, your address switches over to it. Please don’t cancel your old website plan until I tell you the new one is live, and never cancel the address itself or any email you get through that company.

Can I remove your access later?

Yes, any time, on your own. Go to Members, open my name, and choose Revoke. Your site stays up and nothing breaks.

What if I want someone else to work on my site one day?

Everything is in your account, in your name. You’d invite them the same way you invited me. You’re never tied to me.

Will you be able to see my card?

No. The Administrator role can’t see or change billing. That stays with you.

What happens if the address isn’t renewed?

It renews automatically every year unless you turn that off, and you’ll get an email before it does. If it ever lapsed, people couldn’t reach your site at that address until it was renewed.

Can I get an email address with my business name?

Yes. I’ll set up an address like info@yourbusiness.com that forwards to the inbox you already use, free. If you already have business email, it stays exactly as it is. If you later want a separate full mailbox, that’s a paid service we can talk about.

Do I need to understand any of the technical side?

No. Once step 3 is done, you’re finished. If anything on your screen doesn’t match this page, stop and message me rather than guessing. It’s faster for both of us.
